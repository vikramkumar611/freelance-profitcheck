A simple example

For a ₹50,000 project with 10% platform fees, 2% payment fees, ₹3,500 expenses, 0% estimated tax, and 40 hours of work:

₹50,000 − ₹5,000 − ₹1,000 − ₹3,500 = ₹40,500 estimated take-home, or about ₹1,013 per hour. Change any input above to model your own situation.
